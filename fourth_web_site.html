About Me

Hi, my name is Volodimir. I'm a young doctor in computer science. My research is in the intersection of machine learning, statistics and theoretical computer science. I defended my thesis in computer science a few months ago, and since then I'm working as a postdoc in the Inria MALICE team. I'm currently interested in Operator Theory (Neuron Operators, Approximation of the Koopman Operator, etc.).
